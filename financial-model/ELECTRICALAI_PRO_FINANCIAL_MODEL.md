### Lifetime Value (LTV) Calculation

```
Starter Tier:
- Monthly Revenue: $299
- Gross Margin: 91%
- Monthly Churn: 4.2%
- LTV: $299 × 0.91 ÷ 0.042 = $6,477

Professional Tier:
- Monthly Revenue: $899  
- Gross Margin: 89%
- Monthly Churn: 2.1%
- LTV: $899 × 0.89 ÷ 0.021 = $38,138

Enterprise Tier:
- Monthly Revenue: $2,999
- Gross Margin: 93%
- Monthly Churn: 1.4%
- LTV: $2,999 × 0.93 ÷ 0.014 = $199,437

```
