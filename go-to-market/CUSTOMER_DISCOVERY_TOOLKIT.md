# 🎯 ElectricalAI Pro - Customer Discovery Toolkit
## Week 1 Execution Plan

### 📋 TARGET CUSTOMER PROFILE

**Primary Targets (Days 1-3):**
- **Mid-Market Electrical Contractors** ($1M-50M annual revenue)
- **Geographic Focus**: Texas, California, Florida, New York
- **Project Types**: Commercial, industrial, multi-family residential
- **Pain Points**: Bid accuracy, project overruns, labor shortages

**Secondary Targets (Days 4-7):**
- **Larger Regional Contractors** ($50M-500M revenue)
- **Specialty Contractors**: Data centers, hospitals, manufacturing
- **Forward-thinking Owners**: Already using some technology

### 🔍 CUSTOMER DISCOVERY SCRIPT

**Opening Hook (30 seconds):**
```
"Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] from ElectricalAI Pro. I've built an AI system 
that learns from 127,000+ electrical projects to predict bid accuracy. 

Our beta users are seeing 94% estimation accuracy vs. industry average of 
77% - that's preventing $50K+ in overruns per project.

Would you be interested in a 15-minute demo to see how it compares to your 
current estimation process?"
```

**Discovery Questions:**
1. **Current Process**: "Walk me through how you prepare bids today"
2. **Pain Points**: "What's your biggest challenge with estimation accuracy?"
3. **Cost of Error**: "What happens when a project goes over budget?"
4. **Decision Process**: "Who's involved in choosing new software tools?"
5. **Budget**: "What do you currently spend on estimation software?"

**Demo Hook:**
```
"Let me show you something interesting. Here's a project similar to ones 
you've done - our AI predicted $77,185 while traditional methods estimated 
$65,000. The actual cost was $76,892. Want to see how it got so accurate?"
```

### 💡 DEMO STRUCTURE (15 minutes)

**Minutes 1-3: Problem Setup**
- "Show me your last bid that went over budget"
- "What caused the overrun?"
- "How much time did the re-estimation take?"

**Minutes 4-8: AI Solution Demo**
- Upload their floor plan to your system
- Show AI detection of electrical elements
- Generate estimation with Historical Data Analyst
- Compare to their actual costs

**Minutes 9-12: Construction Guide Value**
- Show generated field guide with Grid B3 + (6.5", -2.0") precision
- Demonstrate mobile-friendly format
- Highlight safety protocols and quality checks

**Minutes 13-15: Next Steps**
- "Based on what you've seen, what questions do you have?"
- "Would you be interested in running a pilot on your next project?"
- "What would success look like for your team?"
